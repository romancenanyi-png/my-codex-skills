# Facebook Lead Ads Ops

The primary result is a qualified buyer opportunity, not the lowest cost per lead.

## Define qualification first

Specify country, buyer role, product fit, realistic quantity, buying timeline, and valid business contact. Grade leads:

- **A:** business identity + product/quantity/timing fit + reachable contact.
- **B:** plausible buyer but one important field needs verification.
- **C:** consumer, job seeker, supplier, spam, unsupported market, or non-buying intent.

## Campaign workflow

1. Select one country, one product family, one buyer type, and one conversion route.
2. Build evidence-led creative around one risk. Keep claims within visible or documented proof.
3. Use the same creative/form when comparing audiences; use the same audience/form when comparing video length or hook.
4. Build a higher-intent form with company, work email, phone, country, buyer identity, product, quantity range, timing, and primary concern.
5. Explain the next step on the thank-you screen and verify privacy-policy and contact links.
6. Run long enough for a fair sample—typically seven days or about ten leads—unless a hard error or compliance issue requires stopping.
7. Join ad metrics to A/B/C grades, replies, quotes, and samples. Calculate qualified-lead cost, not only CPL.

## Preflight

Verify mobile preview, form logic, required fields, notifications, privacy URL, destination, tracking, country/language, budget, schedule, and no unsupported promise. Do not mix multiple countries and products in one diagnostic test.

## Output

Return campaign hypothesis, audience, creative variable, form schema, budget/schedule, qualification rubric, event tracking, stop/continue rule, and review table. Publishing or spending requires explicit authorization.
