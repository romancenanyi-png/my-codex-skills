# TikTok B2B Buyer Discovery

Use the chain `TikTok signal → identity → procurement proof → decision maker → contact → dedupe → score`.

## Discovery

Search product/use case with behavior words such as `restock`, `sold out`, `new drop`, `packing orders`, `wholesale inventory`, `vendor sourcing`, `private label`, `bulk order`, `corporate gifts`, or `launch`. Prioritize activity within 30 days; accept 31–90 days only when the current catalog and business are still active.

## Hard gates

1. Match at least three identity elements across TikTok and official sources; official domain and product category are mandatory.
2. Find evidence of repeat inventory, distribution, wholesale, branded assortment, institutional orders, or outsourcing need.
3. Reject personal creators/affiliates, tiny hobby retail without repeat inventory, marketplace/drop-ship only, repair/resale/consignment, trademark-risk accounts, no official site, stale accounts, and guessed/generic-only contacts.
4. Follow the decision-maker ladder: sourcing/procurement → product/merchandising → partnerships → founder/owner for small firms.
5. Dedupe company, domain, handle, email, person, phone, and previous sent history.

## Scoring

Score 100: procurement evidence 25, product fit 20, demand signal 20, contact quality 20, current activity 10, personalization angle 5. Default A threshold is 85.

## Output

Return reviewed-account count, candidate table, A-grade list, rejection log, evidence URLs, observation dates, decision-maker source, contact status, dedupe result, score breakdown, and a one-sentence angle. A typical research funnel may review 80 profiles for 50 candidates and a 15-company pilot; quality gates override quotas.

This skill discovers and verifies. Sending needs separate explicit authority.
