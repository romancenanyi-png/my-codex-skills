# B2B Content Marketing

Use this skill to plan and create content that attracts qualified B2B buyers and moves them toward sales conversations.

## When to use

Use for:

- Content calendars and topic clusters.
- SEO briefs, blog outlines, comparison pages, guides, and pillar pages.
- Case studies, white papers, reports, playbooks, and lead magnets.
- LinkedIn thought-leadership posts and executive content.
- Email nurture sequences and repurposing plans.
- Content for niche B2B, industrial, SaaS, export, and professional-services sites.

## Strategy inputs

- ICP and buying committee.
- Product category and positioning.
- Sales objections and competitor alternatives.
- Target regions and languages.
- SEO priorities and keywords.
- Proof assets: customers, metrics, certifications, case studies.

## Workflow

1. Define content goals: demand generation, organic traffic, sales enablement, trust, retention.
2. Segment by funnel stage and buyer role.
3. Build topic clusters around business problems, use cases, industries, alternatives, and jobs-to-be-done.
4. Map each topic to a page type and conversion offer.
5. Write content briefs with search intent, angle, outline, proof, CTA, internal links, and schema needs.
6. Repurpose pillar content into short-form social, email, sales snippets, and visuals.
7. Track performance and update the calendar.
