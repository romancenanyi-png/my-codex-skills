# B2B Lead Generation

Use this skill to design a complete lead-generation engine from traffic source to sales handoff.

## When to use

Use when the user asks for:

- ICP, account lists, prospecting strategy, or lead scoring.
- Demo/contact/quote forms.
- Lead magnets and gated assets.
- B2B outbound email sequences.
- CRM stages, routing, enrichment, qualification, or lifecycle operations.
- Landing pages meant to capture leads.

## Workflow

1. Define ICP and disqualifiers.
2. Map lead sources: organic, paid, LinkedIn, partners, outbound, directories, events, referrals.
3. Design offers: demo, audit, calculator, white paper, sample, consultation, quote.
4. Design form fields and progressive profiling.
5. Define lead score: fit, intent, engagement, source, firmographics, technographics.
6. Define lifecycle stages: visitor → lead → MQL → SQL → opportunity → customer.
7. Draft follow-up sequences and sales handoff notes.
8. Specify tracking and reporting.
