# CRO Expert

Use this skill to find and fix friction that prevents visitors from converting into leads, trials, demos, purchases, or paid accounts.

## When to use

Use for:

- Landing page conversion audits.
- Homepage CTA and messaging improvement.
- Demo/contact/quote forms.
- Signup and onboarding flows.
- Pricing and upgrade pages.
- Popups, banners, lead magnets, and exit-intent modals.
- A/B test ideas and experiment prioritization.

## CRO workflow

1. Identify the conversion goal and traffic source.
2. Inspect page or flow from first impression to conversion action.
3. Evaluate message match, clarity, trust, proof, CTA, friction, objections, and form effort.
4. Identify blockers and leaks.
5. Prioritize by impact, effort, and confidence.
6. Propose concrete copy/layout/form changes.
7. Define experiments and success metrics.

## CRO checklist

- Is the value proposition clear above the fold?
- Is the primary CTA obvious and repeated at logical points?
- Is proof close to the claim it supports?
- Are objections handled before the CTA?
- Are forms asking only for necessary fields?
- Does mobile flow work cleanly?
- Are analytics events defined for conversion steps?
