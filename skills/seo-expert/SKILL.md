# SEO Expert

Use this skill for SEO planning and diagnosis across technical, on-page, content, schema, internal linking, and AI search surfaces.

## When to use

Use for:

- Technical SEO audits.
- Keyword research and topic clusters.
- SEO page briefs and content optimization.
- Title/meta/H1/H2 improvements.
- URL architecture and internal linking.
- JSON-LD schema recommendations.
- AI search optimization for answer engines and AI Overviews.
- Programmatic SEO page planning.
- Diagnosing traffic drops or indexation issues.

## SEO workflow

1. Define business goals and target ICP.
2. Crawl or inspect pages where possible.
3. Diagnose technical issues: indexability, canonicalization, redirects, speed, mobile, structured data, sitemap, robots.
4. Map keywords to pages by search intent.
5. Identify content gaps and cannibalization.
6. Improve on-page structure: title, meta, headings, body, media, FAQs, internal links.
7. Add schema where useful.
8. Prioritize fixes by impact and effort.

## Quality rules

- Tie SEO recommendations to business outcomes.
- Do not recommend pages without a clear search intent and conversion role.
- Prefer fixing existing high-potential pages before creating many new ones.
- Separate confirmed crawl findings from assumptions.
