# B2B SEO and GEO Ops

Treat GEO as evidence-rich, accessible SEO—not as a magic file, tag, or guaranteed citation tactic.

## Workflow

1. Define one market, buyer type, product family, and business outcome.
2. Gather narrow queries such as product + buyer/use case + OEM/private label/wholesale terms.
3. Inspect current SERPs and classify intent: supplier, product, technical, comparison, compliance, or consumer.
4. Label tool numbers as measured and AI suggestions as candidates. Never invent volume, CPC, or difficulty.
5. Assign every cluster to one canonical URL. Prefer updating a relevant URL over creating a competing page.
6. Build briefs with a direct answer, conditions, proof, buyer questions, internal links, title, meta, H1, and CTA.
7. Keep visible content and structured data equivalent. Schema may describe content; it may not create facts.
8. Publish in controlled batches and observe at 14, 28, 56, and 90 days using GSC, analytics, leads, and assisted conversions.

## Content standard

- State who the page serves, the supported product/capability, boundaries, process, and next step.
- Use real authorship, dates, specifications, test/certification evidence, and sourceable examples where available.
- Link through crawlable HTML between related products, capabilities, resources, FAQs, and inquiry pages.
- Reject consumer/POD traffic and claims the supplier cannot fulfill.

## Output and validation

Return a table with cluster, intent, evidence source, target URL, action (`keep/update/create/merge`), brief, internal links, KPI, and review date. Check indexability, canonical, title, meta, H1, visible answer, links, Schema parity, sitemap presence, and measured results. Report uncertainty; never guarantee ranking, indexing, or AI inclusion.
