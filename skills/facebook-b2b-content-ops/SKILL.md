# Facebook B2B Content Ops

Move from “show the factory” to “help a buyer reduce a purchase risk.”

## Strategy

Define one buyer, one product/use case, one risk question, one proof action, and one CTA per piece. Use a progression from company reality → product map → process → proof → OEM/ODM → use case → comparison → FAQ/objection → buyer-specific solution.

Prioritize content roughly as:

- 60% risk reduction and production proof.
- 25% product structure, use case, and decision guidance.
- 15% trust, quotation education, and company context.

## Short-video structure

For a 20–25 second cold-audience reel:

1. 0–2s: result + named buyer + risk.
2. 2–6s: why the risk matters.
3. 6–16s: visible actions that reduce it.
4. 16–20s: observable result or boundary.
5. 20–25s: one CTA.

Visible action proves only what can be seen. Do not convert visits, inspections, packing, or workshop footage into claims of customer approval, orders, certifications, or defect-free quality.

## Operating loop

1. Audit cover, bio, pinned content, contact path, recent 28-day results, country mix, and content mix.
2. Build a weekly plan; a practical baseline is two reels and one supporting post.
3. Test one variable at a time, especially hooks using the same body and CTA.
4. Track reach, 3-second views, average watch, engagement, follows, leads, qualified leads, quotes, and samples.
5. Share lead quality back to content owners; high views from the wrong market are not success.

## Output

Return buyer/risk brief, evidence boundary, script with timecodes, caption, CTA, publishing checklist, metric hypothesis, and next test. Flag any sentence that requires business verification.
